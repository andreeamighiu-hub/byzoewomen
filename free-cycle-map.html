What's on the map

Four phases. Four completely different versions of you.

Days 1–5

Menstrual

Rest · Reflect · Reset

Energy at its lowest while the body does significant internal work. Not the week to restrict harder — the week to nourish.

Rising estrogen, improving insulin sensitivity. Your highest-return training window of the month — when strength gains stick fastest.
