Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

This Refund & Cancellation Policy applies to purchases of the Zōē Method coaching experience and associated digital products offered by Zōē.

By purchasing the Zōē Method, you acknowledge that you have read and agreed to this Refund & Cancellation Policy in addition to our Terms & Conditions.

2. 30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee

We want every woman joining the Zōē Method to feel confident in her decision.

If you are not satisfied with your purchase, you may submit a written refund request within 30 calendar days from the date of purchase.

Refund requests received after the applicable guarantee period will not normally be eligible for refund unless otherwise required by applicable law.

3. Personalized Coaching Services

The Zōē Method is a personalized coaching experience.

Unlike standard digital products, significant professional time, expertise, and individualized work begin shortly after enrollment. These services are created specifically for each participant and cannot be reassigned or reused.

Where personalized services have already been completed and delivered prior to a refund request, Zōē reserves the right to deduct the value of those completed services from any refund issued, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

Only services that have been completed and delivered prior to the refund request may be deducted from the refundable amount. Services that have not yet been provided will not be charged.

6. Payment Plans

Customers purchasing through the monthly payment option remain responsible for payments already due before an approved cancellation takes effect.

Where a refund is approved, any refund amount will be calculated in accordance with this Refund & Cancellation Policy.

7. How to Request a Refund

Refund requests must be submitted in writing. Please include:

  • Full name

  • Order number (if available)

Requests should be sent to: hello@byzoewomen.com

If your purchase was processed through an authorised payment partner such as Stripe, you may also be required to follow that platform's refund procedures.

8. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be returned using the original payment method whenever possible.

Processing times may vary depending on the payment provider and financial institution.

9. Cancellation of Coaching

Participants may discontinue participation in the coaching experience at any time.

However, discontinuing participation does not automatically entitle the participant to a refund. Refund eligibility will be determined in accordance with this Refund & Cancellation Policy.

10. Payment Processor Policies

Where purchases are processed through third-party payment providers, including Stripe, payment processing, billing procedures, and certain refund procedures may also be governed by those providers' applicable terms and policies.

Nothing in this Refund & Cancellation Policy limits any mandatory consumer rights or protections provided under applicable law.

11. Statutory Rights
