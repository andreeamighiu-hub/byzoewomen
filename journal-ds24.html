What this journal is

Not a blank page.

The Aligned Woman is a 244-page digital journal built around the four phases of the female hormonal cycle — menstrual, follicular, ovulatory and luteal. Every single daily page is unique. The question, the quote, the phase note and the closing line change every day across six complete cycles.

Because you are not the same on day four as you are on day seventeen. Your journal should know the difference.

Each daily page includes a phase header that tells you exactly where you are hormonally, a phase-specific quote, an energy and sleep tracker, a morning reflection question, a permission statement for today, an evening question, and a closing line. Every element is designed for the phase you are in — not the phase you think you should be in.
