Why hormonal health gifts matter

Women's hormonal health has historically been underfunded, under-researched and under-discussed. The average woman spends years navigating PMS, irregular cycles, unexplained fatigue, mood shifts and skin changes without being given an explanation that accounts for her biology. A gift that says "your hormones are real, your cycle matters, and here are tools to understand both" lands differently from a candle or a voucher.

The gifting audience for hormonal health products is wide: partners and husbands who want to better support the women in their lives, mothers buying for daughters, friends who have found cycle syncing transformative, colleagues who want to give something genuinely useful. What all of these buyers have in common is wanting to give something that says "I thought about you."
