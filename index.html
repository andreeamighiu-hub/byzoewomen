The 4-phase shift

Four hormonal phases.
Four completely different
versions of your capacity.

This is the map you were never given. Each phase has its own hormonal environment, its own energy signature, and its own response to training and nutrition.

Days 1 to 5
Menstrual
Rest. Reflect. Reset.

Energy at its lowest. The body is doing significant internal work. This is not the week to restrict harder. It is the week to nourish.

Days 6 to 13
Follicular
Start. Build. Plan.

Rising estrogen. Improving insulin sensitivity. Your highest return training window of the month. This is when strength gains stick fastest.

Days 14 to 16
Ovulatory
Push. Show up. Connect.

Peak estrogen and a brief testosterone rise. Your performance window. Schedule your hardest sessions and highest-visibility work here.

Days 17 to 28
Luteal
Protect. Manage. Stabilise.

Progesterone rises. Core temperature increases. Cravings are physiological, not weakness. The phase that rewards intelligence over ego.
