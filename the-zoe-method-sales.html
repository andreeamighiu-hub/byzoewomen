Questions

Everything you
might be wondering.

Does this work if I'm on the pill?
Yes. This methodology is built around your female biology — your physiology, your energy patterns, your nutritional needs as a woman — not solely around a natural hormonal cycle. Whether you're on hormonal contraception or not, the training and nutrition approach is designed to work with your body as it is. Your onboarding call gives us everything we need to build your plan accordingly.
How personalized is the plan, really?
Completely. Your onboarding call captures your goals, current fitness level, lifestyle, training history, and how your body responds. Francesco builds your plan from that specific information — it is not a template with your name on it. Every four weeks it's reviewed and updated based on how you've actually responded. This is real coaching, not a downloaded PDF.
What is Mr Z and how does it work?
Mr Z is an AI coaching assistant built on ZTraining's 18-year methodology, available 24/7 on Telegram. You can ask it anything in real time — how to adjust today's session if you're tired, whether to eat more before training, what to do if you missed a workout. Your personal access code arrives by email within 24 hours of joining. From the moment you purchase, you can also access Mr Z directly at t.me/AskMrZAI_bot with 60 free messages while you wait.
How is the coaching delivered and when does it begin?
Everything in The Zōē Method is delivered digitally — no physical items are shipped. Following your purchase, you will receive a confirmation email within a few minutes with instructions to schedule your introductory consultation with Andreea. Please check your spam folder if you don't see it. Your personalised training and nutrition plan is built by Francesco after your onboarding call and delivered to you by email. Coaching adjustments and all communications throughout the three months happen by email. Mr Z AI support is accessed via Telegram.

Your final review call with Andreea covers what changed, what you've learned, and what's next. Mr Z access is included for your coaching duration (3 months); continuation options are available after that. You keep the Women's Hormone Blueprint and the Aligned Woman Journal permanently.

Because this journey involves real human coaching — Francesco writing and adjusting personal plans, Andreea conducting onboarding and final calls. Genuine attention has a capacity. No. The Zōē Method is an educational and coaching journey. Andreea is a women's hormonal health educator, not a physician. The methodology is designed to complement, not replace, personalised medical advice. If you have a specific health condition, please consult your doctor before joining.
