No brand bidding. Don't bid on "Zōē," "byzoewomen," "ZTraining," the product name or misspellings in paid search.

Women-focused placement. Keep traffic within female fitness, coaching, nutrition and hormonal-health contexts.

Cohort capacity is real. Only reference the 100-spot cap and cohort timing if it's accurate at the time you're promoting, check with us if you're running a larger campaign so we can confirm current availability.
